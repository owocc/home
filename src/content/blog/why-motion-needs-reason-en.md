Motion is captivating in web development, but it carries a silent risk: movement naturally commands attention, even when devoid of meaning. Before introducing an animation, we must ask what concept it is actually explaining.

## The Three Fundamental Roles

1. **Feedback**: Tactile confirmation like subtle push-back on button taps.
2. **Spatial Explanation**: Guiding mental models, such as expanding cards into full modals.
3. **Choreographing Focus**: Prioritizing newly arrived information smoothly into the viewport.

When motion has purpose, the interface feels alive rather than distracting.
